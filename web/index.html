    Monthly mode completes all Psalms and Proverbs each calendar month, including February. Other modes use independent 30- and 31-reading cycles. The annual part is a simple Genesis-to-Revelation plan, not an official ESV plan. Applying a plan resets both parts. Progress stays in this browser on this device.
